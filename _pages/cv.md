## Research profile

Computational scientist developing hybrid physics–machine learning methods that integrate differentiable simulation and high-performance computing (HPC) to accelerate physics-based simulations and enable accurate, scalable modeling, prediction, and control of complex fluid systems.

## Technical expertise

<p><strong>Research frameworks:</strong> Diff-NekRS for differentiable spectral-element simulations; physics-informed B-spline flow-data reconstruction; weak-form corrections for hybrid finite-element simulations; agentic orchestration for stable simulations.</p>

<p><strong>Numerical and data-driven methods:</strong> CFD (DNS, LES, RANS); spectral-element and finite-element methods; flow estimation and control; DMD, POD, SPOD, resolvent analysis.</p>

<p><strong>Scientific ML:</strong> Deep learning (CNN, GNN, U-Net, Transformer); agentic workflows for simulations.</p>

<p><strong>Programming and parallel computing:</strong> C/C++, Python, Fortran; MPI and parallel implementation (OpenMPI, MPICH); GPU and heterogeneous computing; Git/GitHub, Ubuntu, and Bash.</p>

<p><strong>Scientific libraries and computing environments:</strong> PETSc, FFTW, LAPACK; DOE, DoD, and university supercomputing environments.</p>
